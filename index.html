DISCLAIMER

The following images have been collected from the declassified documents of the CIA, detailing experiments and proposals for using animals as instruments for spying. Recent developments allowed scientists to retrieve creatures from older historical times. These creatures retrieved from previous points of our timeline, can be hihgly useful assets in spy operations for the CIA. The docments on this page show creatures from medieval times, used as assets for CIA operations.

The assortment of images and documents is a commentary on the complex relations of humans, animals, and technology. From the perspective of humans, the animals with the cameras or other technological objects on them, pose as objects with hermeneutical or interpretive relationships to the human, as the human observes the world or interprets it through the images produced by the animal and camera. In this case, the animal and the technological object become one technological instrument. However, from the perspective of the animal, the technological object is merely a background element of their reality, that is noticed, but not interacted with in the classical sense. However, as objects have different affordances for different species, it can be argued that the animal also forms a relationship with the technological object, it is just a different one, from the human affordance.
